# Three-minute demo

Use the no-sign-in guided path. Select **Live AI** while Groq is available; otherwise select **Replay** and say so explicitly.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

Open the landing page and select **Launch live demo**.

> Companies are starting to let agents purchase compute and services, but a contract says who may be paid, how much and how often. Existing agent wallets do not understand that commercial intent. FluxMandate turns it into bounded, explainable authority.

## 0:20–0:50 — Contract interpretation

Extract the synthetic Axiom Compute Services Agreement.

> Groq proposes the counterparty, total commitment, payment schedule, term and renewal control. The server validates the shape and every visible result remains a proposal. The AI cannot approve or sign anything.

Point out: Axiom Compute Labs; USD 12,000 total; USD 4,000 every 30 days; three-month term; renewal requires human approval.

## 0:50–1:15 — Human checkpoint

Review the evidence cards and approve the terms.

> A person checks the commercial meaning before it can become authority. This is the boundary between probabilistic interpretation and deterministic finance.

## 1:15–1:40 — FluxTwin

Show cash, committed spend, runway and safe-to-deploy capacity.

> These figures come from deterministic code, not an AI explanation. The same inputs produce the same result.

## 1:40–2:10 — Bounded mandate

Show the vendor, asset, cap, agent and expiry.

> The approved terms become a revocable authority envelope. The deployed Anchor program records contract commitments and mandate state and enforces the payment cap. This screen is a guided representation of the recorded Devnet run—not a new wallet transaction.

## 2:10–2:35 — Both sides of the boundary

Try USD 1,260, then USD 5,200.

> The first request is within the USD 4,000 cap. The second exceeds it and is refused with a reason. The interactive buttons exercise the deterministic replay path; the Proof step links the separate recorded Devnet transfer and on-chain rejection.

## 2:35–3:00 — Proof and close

Open the trace.

> FluxProof connects contract, obligation, mandate, agent, settlement and ledger evidence. AI interprets. Humans approve. Code calculates. Solana enforces. That is FluxMandate.
